THE GAME
---------
- Original top-down 2D stealth assassin game for Android.
- Genre inspiration: Hunter Assassin (Ruby Games) — but this is NOT that game.
  Everything is ours: own name, own art, own code, own levels, own characters.
  Nothing copied — no copyright risk.
- Theme: assassin kept (Buddhika's choice, 2026-11-21).
- Cartoonish style, 7+ audience.

LOOK (v3 — brighter "contrast" palette, NOT premium 3D)
-------------------------------------------------------
- Light cool-grey concrete floors (luminance 183-194), dark slate-blue brick
  walls, amber X-brace crates — the board is much brighter and easier to read
  than v2's dark warehouse. Player + 8 guard sprites redrawn: facing wedge,
  distinct colours per guard.
- WHITE flashlight cones, visible above the floor (amber when suspicious,
  red when alerted).
- Themes change per level band: warehouse -> cyber city -> lab -> prison.
- NOTE (2026-11-21): Buddhika asked for a PREMIUM 3D look. That overhaul was
  attempted twice and both attempts were blocked, so it is NOT in this build.
  The research for it (techniques + free code from teaching sites) is saved at
  docs/reference/premium-2d-look-research-2026-11-21.md in the repo.

GAMEPLAY (v3 — what changed since v2)
--------------------------------------
- MOVEMENT FIXED (Buddhika's #1 complaint in v2: he tapped/dragged and the
  player stayed in one place). Root cause: the full-screen backdrop ColorRect
  had the default mouse_filter=STOP, so it swallowed every tap before the
  input handler ever ran. Fixed: input now goes through _input, the backdrop
  is MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE, and touch / drag / mouse click all move the player.
  The tiny bottom hint text is gone; a big banner now says
  "Get the KEY, then reach the EXIT!" at the start of every level.
  Verified with real injected input (touch, drag, mouse click) — the player
  walks the drawn line at once.
- KEY + EXIT WIN CONDITION (new — Buddhika's v2 feedback): every level now has
  a gold KEY near the guards' patrol zone and a bright-green EXIT square on
  the far side, with a bouncing green arrow pointing down at it. Win = collect
  the key, then reach the exit. Reaching the exit WITHOUT the key does NOT win
  — it shows "LOCKED — get the KEY first!". The HUD has a key icon that turns
  from grey to gold when the key is collected. You do NOT have to kill every
  guard any more — but killing them is how you reach the key safely.
- WIDE PATROLS (Buddhika's v2 feedback: guards only patrolled one small area).
  Guards now walk long A*-routed loops all over the level (L1: 16+16 tiles,
  was 4+2; L50: 65 route tiles over 9 guards), so the player must watch,
  time moves, and sneak up behind them.
- WEAPONS (new): Knife (free, silent, contact kill — walk into a guard) and
  Gun (ranged, LOUD, 0.9s cooldown, tap a guard to shoot from up to 5 tiles;
  unlock for 500 coins or by watching a rewarded ad). Side weapon UI dock on
  the right edge of the screen; the gun has no contact kill. Unlock is saved.
- HOW TO PLAY POPUP FIXED (Buddhika's feedback 2026-11-21 16:16: the popup was a
  full-screen panel with very long text and NO way to close it — the player was
  stuck and could not even reach the Play button). Root cause: the long text
  pushed the "Got it!" button off the bottom of the 680px panel. Fixed: the
  text is now 5 short lines, there is a red X button top-right, and tapping
  ANYWHERE on the popup dismisses it. Verified 5/5 checks under xvfb.
- V4 (Buddhika's feedback 2026-11-21 17:38 + 17:42 + 18:15): (1) FULL-SCREEN
  BOARD — levels are now 11x20 with adaptive tile size, so the board fills
  tall phones (720x1640) with no black band at the bottom. (2) BOTTOM CONTROL
  BAR — Pause, Knife and Gun are now three picture buttons (drawn PNG icons,
  never emoji) in a bar at the bottom of the screen; tap to switch weapons
  mid-level, locked gun opens the unlock shop, active weapon gets a gold
  border. (3) RICHER 2D GRAPHICS — more detailed sprites with highlights,
  shading and warm rim light; brighter floors/walls. (4) FIRST-TIME TUTORIAL
  — in Levels 1-2 a drawn pointing hand + short text + Skip button teaches
  move / key / exit / weapon switch / pause when each feature first matters;
  the gun-unlock hint appears when the player reaches 500 coins (even in
  later levels). Hints never block play and are never shown twice (stored in
  save data). (5) HOW TO PLAY IS NOW A SMALL SQUARE WINDOW (480x480) with a
  red X at the top-right and 5 short lines — tap the X or anywhere to close.
- V5 (Buddhika's v4 playtest feedback, 2026-11-21 20:11): (1) COIN COUNTER
  IN THE HUD — the gold coin icon + number of coins collected now sits in
  the top bar next to the gems, and it climbs by 5 the moment a coin is
  picked up (Buddhika: collected coins were nowhere to be seen). (2) THE
  CONTACT-KILL RULE IS FIXED — before, ANY touch killed the guard for
  free, even standing still or a guard walking up from behind. Now the
  player kills a guard ONLY by walking into them (best from behind — a
  stealth kill); if a guard walks into the player, or the player walks
  into a guard who faces them, the GUARD hurts the player instead, and a
  guard who spots the player chases and attacks — the player CAN die when
  caught, no more free wins by standing still. (3) GUN GUARDS ARE NOW
  LETHAL — bullets damage the player (3 hearts -> death); walking into a
  shooting guard's gunfire gets you killed, so the knife is for stealth
  (behind) and the gun is for distance. (4) HOMEPAGE REDESIGNED —
  purple-to-teal gradient, gold trim, scattered coins + gems, the title in
  gold and teal, coin/gem wallet chips, and a BIG green PLAY button with a
  gold frame front and centre (tap it and the game starts at once);
  How to Play is now a SMALL button in the top-right corner, Sound in the
  top-left; ASSASSINS and the 50-level grid are still right there below.
  The small-square How to Play window (480x480, red X, 5 short lines) is
  unchanged.
- Top-down 2D view, whole level on one screen; tap-to-move + drag-to-steer
  with an orange path line and tap ring.
- Contact kills: sneak up behind a guard and walk into them to eliminate
  them (blood pool, cash pop, +N popup, hit-stop, screen shake, kill
  sound). A guard who walks into you, or one you meet face to face, hurts
  you instead — and a spotted guard chases and shoots.
- Guards shoot on sight (tracer bullets) + player health pool (3 hearts,
  brief invulnerability after a hit). Death -> Revive (50 gems, 5s countdown)
  or game over.
- Kill noise: guards nearby converge on the kill, turn around randomly,
  red blips at the screen edge; they calm down after ~2.5s.
- 6 enemy types: basic, rifle (destroys crates), shotgun, grenade, sniper,
  shielded (introduced gradually: rifle L3, shotgun L5, grenade L8, sniper
  L20, shielded L30).
- Multikill banners: "DOUBLE KILL!", "TRIPLE KILL!", "RAMPAGE!".
- Traps from L15: bushes to hide in, bombs, cryo mines.
- Coins (cash) + gems to collect; gems buy 4 assassins (Shadow free,
  Ninja 150, Ronin 300, Ghost 800). HUD: target icon + 0/N, hearts, gems,
  COINS (gold coin icon + number), key icon.
- LOTS of levels: 50 levels, all validated beatable (50/50, 0 problems).
  Guard count: L1=2, L2=3, L3=3, mid 4, L10=6, L50=9. Dense mazes.
- Difficulty: the first levels are EASY; each level gets a little bit
  harder than the one before — more guards, faster patrols, wider vision
  cones, tighter spaces, trickier layouts.
- Every level is BEATABLE — the player can win all the way to the last
  level — but the later levels are hard and need real thinking: watch the
  patrols, time your moves, plan your route. Hard, never impossible.
- Music loop + richer sound effects (kill, shoot, alert, cash, multikill,
  win, lose).
